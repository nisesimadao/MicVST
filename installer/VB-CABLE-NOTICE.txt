VB-CABLE third-party notice
===========================

MicVST uses VB-CABLE as its virtual microphone transport on Windows.
VB-CABLE is made by VB-Audio Software and is NOT part of MicVST.

VB-CABLE is distributed by VB-Audio as donationware.
If you find VB-CABLE useful, please consider supporting VB-Audio.

Official site:
https://vb-cable.com/

Licensing / distribution terms:
https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

MicVST does not rename, modify, or re-sign the VB-CABLE driver. The official
VB-Audio package is downloaded from VB-Audio's own server and installed as-is.

MicVST routes processed microphone audio to the playback endpoint named
"CABLE Input". Applications such as Discord, OBS, Zoom, and browsers should use
"CABLE Output" as the microphone input.

MicVST intentionally leaves VB-CABLE installed when MicVST is uninstalled,
because VB-CABLE is a shared third-party component that another application may
also be using.
